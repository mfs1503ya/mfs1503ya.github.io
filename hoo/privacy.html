Privacy policy

Hoo · Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short: your dream journal lives on your phone, encrypted, and we never see it. Only the text of a dream you choose to interpret is sent to our server, used once to write the interpretation, and not stored. No ads, no selling of data.

Who we are

Hoo is made by Marc Férriz Sánchez, an independent developer in Barcelona, Spain, who is the data controller. Contact: hooapp@proton.me.

Legal bases (EU and UK users)

Providing the app and the interpretations you ask for (performance of a contract, GDPR art. 6.1.b); counting free use and preventing abuse (legitimate interest, art. 6.1.f); anonymous usage analytics (legitimate interest, which you can object to with the switch in Settings); the Android launch list (your consent, art. 6.1.a, which you can withdraw at any time). Dreams can reveal sensitive things about you: you decide what to write and what to send for interpretation, and sending a dream is your explicit choice (art. 9.2.a).

International transfers

Supabase, Anthropic, RevenueCat and PostHog process data in the USA, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long

Your journal stays on your phone until you delete it in the app (Settings › Delete all my data) or uninstall Hoo. Deleting your data in the app also deletes the anonymous ID and usage counts on our server (with an active Hoo Plus subscription, only the ID linked to it is kept so the subscription keeps working). Device codes are kept only for the current month. Interpretation counters reset every month. Subscription records are kept while the subscription is active and afterwards for as long as the law requires.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data for advertising, there are no ads, and we make no automated decisions with legal effects on you.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing by writing to hooapp@proton.me. We reply within one month. You can export your journal yourself in Settings › Your data. In the EU you can complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, aepd.es). California residents have the rights of the CCPA/CPRA; we do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Hoo is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and tell you in the app before the change applies.
