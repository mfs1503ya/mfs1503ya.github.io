Delete your data

Hoo · Last updated: November 5, 2026

Hoo has no user accounts with name or email. Your journal is stored only on your phone.

How to delete everything

  1. In Hoo, open Settings › Delete all my data and confirm. This erases your journal, interpretations and settings on the phone, and the anonymous ID and usage counts on our server. If you have an active Hoo Plus subscription, we keep only the anonymous ID linked to it, so your subscription keeps working; write to us if you want it deleted too.
  2. Or uninstall Hoo: the phone deletes the journal. In that case the anonymous usage counts on our server are not linked to you and expire with the monthly reset; write to us if you want them removed.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Purchase records

Apple or Google and our provider RevenueCat keep the record of a purchase. To ask us to delete what RevenueCat holds, write to hooapp@proton.me with the store and purchase date. We do it within one month, except what the law requires us to keep (for example, for tax).
